About — Calcpad-Symbolic

About

Calcpad-Symbolic is a fork of CalcpadCE, which is itself a fork of Calcpad by Proektsoftbg. It adds a symbolic-math stack (AngouriMath + Maxima), C++/WASM FEM and interactive visualizations, aimed at structural engineering.

Architecture

  • Symbolic.Core — parser and engine (C#, .NET 10)
  • Symbolic.Cli — .cpd → .html/.docx/.pdf converter
  • Symbolic.Wpf — desktop editor with syntax highlighting
  • calcpad-web-blazor — online editor (Blazor WebAssembly)
  • FEM C++ — Eigen 3.4.0 solvers compiled to WASM
